# NYC Labor Supply Analysis

This repository contains the code for a data story on labor force participation 
in New York City over the past decade, using American Community Survey (ACS) 
PUMS microdata from 2014 to 2024.

The full piece is published here:
[New York City Recovered Its Jobs. Not All Its Workers Did.](https://criticalfields.substack.com/p/76571c6b-372e-452f-ab98-53058e987307)

## What this project covers

The analysis looks at who is — and who isn't — participating in New York City's 
labor market, and how that changed before, during, and after the pandemic. The 
main findings center on three overlapping patterns: the widening credential gap, 
the unequal recovery across racial groups within education levels, and the growing 
share of older New Yorkers staying in the workforce longer than they otherwise would.

## Data sources

- **ACS PUMS 1-Year** (2014–2024): individual-level microdata from the U.S. Census 
  Bureau, accessed via IPUMS. Used for all labor force participation and employment 
  rate calculations.
- **NYC Current Employment Statistics (CES)**: monthly employment levels for New 
  York City, sourced from the NYC Department of Labor.

The raw data files are not included in this repository due to size. ACS PUMS data 
can be downloaded from [IPUMS USA](https://usa.ipums.org/usa/) and CES data from 
the [NYC Department of Labor](https://www.labor.ny.gov/stats/nyc/).
